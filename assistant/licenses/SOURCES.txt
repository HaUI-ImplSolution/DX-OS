Sources of the software in the dxos-assistant image

The image is built from DX-OS (Apache-2.0, https://github.com/HaUI-ImplSolution/DX-OS, the Dockerfile is assistant/Dockerfile) on the base image node:24.16.0-alpine.
The image holds the assistant service, its libraries (notices in THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt) and Node.js without npm and yarn (MIT with the licenses of its bundled parts in /usr/local/LICENSE, source at https://github.com/nodejs/node/tree/v24.16.0).
The classifier weights in /app/classifiers (onestop.json, support.json and clinic.json) are trained by the DX-OS team and are under Apache-2.0 (LICENSE).
They were trained on sentences under CC-BY-4.0: sentences written by the team or drafted by Claude (Anthropic), and sentences from MASSIVE vi-VN 1.0 (Amazon), whose attribution is in NOTICE.
The training data is not in the image; the code, the data, the data card and the training command are in tools/classifier of DX-OS.
The base image holds Alpine Linux v3.24, whose package sources are in the aports tree at https://git.alpinelinux.org/aports (branch 3.24-stable) and in the source packages next to the binary packages at https://dl-cdn.alpinelinux.org/alpine/v3.24/.
Some of these packages are under the GNU General Public License, the GNU Lesser General Public License and similar licenses.
Their names, versions and licenses are listed for every image in docs/IMAGE_PACKAGES.md of DX-OS (https://github.com/HaUI-ImplSolution/DX-OS/blob/main/docs/IMAGE_PACKAGES.md).
For any component of this image under such a license, the maintainers give the source to anyone who asks for it, for three years after the release of the image:
open an issue at https://github.com/HaUI-ImplSolution/DX-OS/issues naming the image, its tag and the component.
