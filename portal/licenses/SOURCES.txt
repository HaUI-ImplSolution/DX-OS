Sources of the software in the dxos-portal image

The image is built from DX-OS (Apache-2.0, https://github.com/HaUI-ImplSolution/DX-OS, the Dockerfile is portal/Dockerfile) on the base image nginx:1.30-alpine-slim.
The image holds the page of the portal and nginx (BSD-2-Clause, source at https://github.com/nginx/nginx).
The base image holds Alpine Linux v3.24, whose package sources are in the aports tree at https://git.alpinelinux.org/aports (branch 3.24-stable) and in the source packages next to the binary packages at https://dl-cdn.alpinelinux.org/alpine/v3.24/.
Some of these packages are under the GNU General Public License, the GNU Lesser General Public License and similar licenses.
Their names, versions and licenses are listed for every image in docs/IMAGE_PACKAGES.md of DX-OS (https://github.com/HaUI-ImplSolution/DX-OS/blob/main/docs/IMAGE_PACKAGES.md).
For any component of this image under such a license, the maintainers give the source to anyone who asks for it, for three years after the release of the image:
open an issue at https://github.com/HaUI-ImplSolution/DX-OS/issues naming the image, its tag and the component.
