Sources of the software in the dxos-sso image

The image is built from DX-OS (Apache-2.0, https://github.com/HaUI-ImplSolution/DX-OS, the Dockerfile is sso/Dockerfile) on the base image eclipse-temurin:21-jre-jammy.
The image holds Keycloak 26.8.2 unpacked from its release archive (Apache-2.0, source at https://github.com/keycloak/keycloak/tree/26.8.2; the libraries in it and their notices are in THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES-keycloak.txt) and OpenJDK from Eclipse Temurin (GPL-2.0 with the Classpath exception, source at https://github.com/adoptium/jdk21u).
The base image holds Ubuntu 22.04 (jammy), whose package sources are at https://archive.ubuntu.com/ubuntu/pool/ and can be fetched with apt-get source on a machine that has the same package versions.
Some of these packages are under the GNU General Public License, the GNU Lesser General Public License and similar licenses.
Their names, versions and licenses are listed for every image in docs/IMAGE_PACKAGES.md of DX-OS (https://github.com/HaUI-ImplSolution/DX-OS/blob/main/docs/IMAGE_PACKAGES.md).
For any component of this image under such a license, the maintainers give the source to anyone who asks for it, for three years after the release of the image:
open an issue at https://github.com/HaUI-ImplSolution/DX-OS/issues naming the image, its tag and the component.
